## How to use this archive

1. Choose a category from the index instead of scanning one flat list.
2. Open the original resource link directly from its row.
3. Verify the destination's current owner, availability, browser support, privacy behavior, price, and license.
4. Treat closed or redirected services as historical evidence, not a recommendation to find an unofficial mirror.
